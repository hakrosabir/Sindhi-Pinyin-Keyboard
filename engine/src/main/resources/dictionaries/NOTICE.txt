The original sd.dic, sd.aff, LGPL.txt, desc.txt and description.xml files are
redistributed unchanged from dict-sd.oxt. Original source descriptions and
attributions, including its stated Urdu dictionary ancestry, are preserved.
The extension advertises 10,000+ Sindhi words and explicitly is not complete.

The keyboard filters entries and builds a phonetic retrieval index in memory.
Roman aliases, vowel-folding, consonant-folding and ranking are independent
project heuristics, not pronunciations provided or validated by the dictionary.
Original output spellings are retained. No corpus frequency is inferred.

You can inspect or replace the dictionary data and rebuild the application.
The source files are included as readable APK assets and in the source tree.
The new independent application code remains Apache-2.0; the dictionary data
retains its own LGPL-2.1 terms.
